# Last-mile cost band research

Checked 1 October 2026. These are survey response bands designed around published fare examples, not a tariff or fare prediction. Keep zero cost, lower/upper bands and exact entry available. No band is preselected for paid transport. Changing mode clears the previous cost.

- Kedarnath: https://www.euttaranchal.com/tourism/kedarnath-ponies-and-palkies-rates.php (published 1 August 2026). Lists Gaurikund–Kedarnath base camp pony at ₹3,500 one way; dandi at ₹9,200–₹15,200 and kandi at ₹6,000–₹8,000 depending on weight. These anchor separate pony, palki and porter bands.
- Yamunotri: https://www.sacredyatra.com/ponies-palkies-rates-for-yamunotri-dham.html (no clear tariff year). Lists one-way pony ₹1,110–₹1,310, kandi ₹760–₹1,260 and dandi ₹2,740–₹4,240; https://www.shivgangatravels.com/yamunotri-pony-palki-rates reports different 2026 estimates. Bands deliberately span both reports.
- Hemkund: https://www.euttaranchal.com/tourism/porter-horse-rates-for-ghangharia-hemkund-valley-of-flowers.php lists Ghangharia–Hemkund one-way pony ₹1,900–₹2,500. Its ₹9,850–₹12,250 dandi figures are RETURN fares, and were not treated as one-way amounts. Broad palki bands are used because a reliable one-way chart was not established and the survey route can include additional legs.

Helicopter and other modes use broad generic collection bands; no verified current tariff is claimed for those modes. Cost band selections are exported separately from exact numeric amounts, without assigning a midpoint.
